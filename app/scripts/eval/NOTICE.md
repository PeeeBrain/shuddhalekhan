# Svarah attribution

The transcript references in `dev-set.json` are a 200-clip subset of
[Svarah: An Indic Accented English Speech Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Svarah)
by AI4Bharat, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The subset adds clip IDs, local filenames, and audio hashes; transcript text is retained from the dataset.

Citation: Tahir Javed, Sakshi Joshi, Vignesh Nagarajan, Sai Sundaresan,
Janki Nawale, Abhigyan Raman, Kaushal Santosh Bhogale, Pratyush Kumar,
and Mitesh M. Khapra. "Svarah: Evaluating English ASR Systems on Indian
Accents." INTERSPEECH 2023, pages 5087–5091.
